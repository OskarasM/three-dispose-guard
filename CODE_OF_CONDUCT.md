# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person on the other end is acting in good faith, is
busier than you think, and knows something you do not.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, in pull requests, in commit messages and in
any discussion that represents the project.

## What is expected

- Argue about the code, the measurement or the design, not about the person.
- Say what you actually mean, and say it plainly. Directness is welcome.
  Contempt is not, and they are not the same thing.
- If you are asserting that something is slow, broken or wrong, bring the
  reproduction. This project publishes its own negative results; you are held
  to the same standard and no higher.
- Accept that maintainer time is finite. An unanswered issue is usually a busy
  week, not a snub.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination on any grounds, deliberate
intimidation, sexualised language or imagery, publishing someone's private
information, and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately by opening a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/OskarasM/three-dispose-guard/security/advisories/new),
which is private between you and the maintainer, or by contacting the
maintainer through the profile at https://github.com/OskarasM.

Reports are read by the maintainer, Oskaras Margevicius. You will get an
acknowledgement within seven days. If your report concerns the maintainer, say
so and it will be handled by disclosure on the public issue tracker rather than
privately, so that it is not judged by the person it is about.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and pattern: a private word, a public correction, having
a comment or commit edited or removed, a temporary block, or a permanent ban.
The maintainer will say which is being applied and why.
